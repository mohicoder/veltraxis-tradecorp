# VELTRAXIS TRADECORP website — project notes

Domain: veltraxistradecorp.com (registered at Hostinger). `www.veltraxistradecorp.com` is the primary; the bare domain redirects to it.
Repo: github.com/mohicoder/veltraxis-tradecorp (branch `main`)
Hosting: Vercel, in its own team (separate from any other business). Every push to `main` deploys to production automatically (~2 min).
VELTRAXIS TRADECORP is an independent business: do not link it to, or mention, any other company on the site.

## Stack
Next.js 15 (App Router) · TypeScript · Tailwind 3 · lucide-react · nodemailer · zod. Fonts self-hosted via @fontsource: Geist (all text; headings set tight with `.h-display`) and Instrument Serif italic for single accent words (`.accent` / `.accent-ink`; in titles passed to `SectionHeading`/`PageHero`, wrap words in *asterisks*). Look: ink-black hero/header/footer, warm off-white sections, champagne gold accents, hairline-bordered grids. Two languages: English at `/…`, Russian at `/ru/…` (Arabic is the planned next language). Russian accent words fall back to Cormorant Garamond italic (Instrument Serif has no Cyrillic).

## Where things live
- `lib/contacts.ts` — **single source of truth** for brand/legal name, GSTIN, IEC, address, phones, email and the inbox the form delivers to. Fill `gstin` / `iec` to show them on the Quality page (empty = "Shared on request").
- `content/en.ts` — all English copy and UI text (from the client's website script, Oct 2026). Exports `en` and `type Dict`.
- `content/ru.ts` — Russian, typed as `Dict`: every key added to `en.ts` must be added here or the build fails. **Always update both languages.** Use `{mb}` placeholders, not functions (dictionaries are passed to client components).
- `app/[lang]/…` — pages; `middleware.ts` rewrites unprefixed URLs to `/en/…` and sets `x-site-lang` (used by the 404 page). Build links with `lp(lang, path)` from `lib/i18n.ts`.
- The enquiry form shows the visitor's language but always emails English labels/values (`tEn`); Russian submissions get a Russian auto-reply and an `[RU]` subject tag.
- `lib/site.ts` — navigation, footer links, routes (sitemap), site URL.
- `components/Globe.tsx` — canvas 3D globe in the home hero (dotted land from `lib/globe-data.ts`, generated by `npm run globe`; routes from India to the cities listed at the top of the file). Pauses off-screen, static with reduced motion.
- `components/RouteMap.tsx` — flat animated map on /markets; dots come from `lib/map-data.ts`, generated by `npm run map` (scripts/build-map.mjs, world-atlas). Edit city list in RouteMap.
- `components/LandedCostCalculator.tsx` — FOB/CIF/duty/tax calculator (home + /landed-cost-calculator).
- `components/EnquiryForm.tsx` — buyer / supplier / partner form; `?product=` and `?role=` prefill it.
- `app/api/enquiry/route.ts` — validates, honeypot, rate limit (6/10 min/IP), attachments, emails `enquiryInbox` + auto-reply.
- `components/Logo.tsx` — official logo (supplied Nov 2026): `public/brand/mark.webp` (emblem, header), `public/brand/logo.webp` (full lockup, footer), `public/brand/og.jpg` (link previews), `app/icon.png` + `app/apple-icon.png`. All cut out of the original dark JPEG for dark backgrounds.
